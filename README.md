# Swiss CryptoTax

Swiss CryptoTax GmbH advises on tax and accounting for cryptocurrencies and other crypto assets.
We work for individuals, companies, startups, asset managers, family offices and tax consultants.

## Services

- Declaration of cryptocurrencies
- Staking, DeFi, airdrops, NFTs and mining
- Risk analysis of professional crypto trading
- Analysis of location-specific and cantonal tax benefits
- Assessment and preparation of crypto transactions
- Fiduciary services

The full list is on [cryptotax.ch](https://www.cryptotax.ch/en/services/).
